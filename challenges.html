Kaggle MedGemma Impact Challenge Submitted
CXR-Sentinel · Multi-Agent Adversarial Validation · Radiology AI Assurance
The submission
CXR-Sentinel: a multi-agent adversarial validation framework for radiology AI built on Google's MedGemma. Independent AI agents cross-validate chest X-ray interpretations, generate targeted perturbations to stress-test robustness, and escalate uncertain cases to human review. Submitted before the February 24, 2026 deadline.
MedGemma Multi-Agent Adversarial Validation Chest X-Ray MIMIC-CXR AI Assurance
Key Metric — RadGraph Entity Agreement
$$\mathrm{DCR} = \frac{|\mathcal{E}_{\text{primary}} \cap \mathcal{E}_{\text{challenger}}|}{|\mathcal{E}_{\text{primary}} \cup \mathcal{E}_{\text{challenger}}|}$$
Diagnostic Concordance Rate — Jaccard similarity of RadGraph entities extracted from independent agent readings. Text-only DCR = 74.6% vs. multimodal DCR = 73.8%.
Architecture
A primary reader generates CXR interpretations via MedGemma, an adversarial challenger generates perturbations (rephrased clinical context, cropped regions, synthetic noise), and a sentinel judge evaluates cross-reading consistency. Cases below agreement threshold are escalated to human review. A counterintuitive finding: text-only analysis slightly outperformed multimodal — reframed as evidence that independent verification channels add assurance value.

Obesity ML Competition Active
CrunchDAO · Broad Institute · Single-Cell Genomics · CRISPR Perturbation Prediction
The challenge
Predicting how CRISPR/Cas9 gene perturbations influence adipocyte cell fate – shifting fat cells between energy-storing and energy-burning states. Hosted by the Eric and Wendy Schmidt Center at the Broad Institute in collaboration with CrunchDAO. Top predictions will be experimentally validated in the lab. Running December 2025 – April 2026.
Single-Cell RNA-seq CRISPR/Cas9 Adipocyte Differentiation Perturbation Prediction AnnData / h5ad CrunchDAO
Perturbation Prediction Objective
$$\hat{p}_g = f_\theta\!\left(X_{\text{train}},\, g\right) \in \Delta^{K-1}$$
Predict the program proportion vector for a held-out gene perturbation g, where K is the number of cell programs and Δ is the probability simplex.
$$\mathcal{L} = D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(\hat{p}_g \,\|\, p_g^*\right) + \lambda\, \mathrm{MMD}^2\!\left(\hat{X}_g,\, X_g^*\right)$$
Combined loss: KL divergence on program proportions + Maximum Mean Discrepancy on expression distributions.
Crunch 1 — Held-Out Gene Perturbations
The first round focuses on predicting single-cell transcriptomic responses to single-gene perturbations not seen in training. The dataset contains perturbations targeting 157 genes (150 transcription factors), with single-cell expression profiles measured at day 14 of adipocyte differentiation. The task: given a held-out gene, predict the resulting expression profile distribution across cell programs.
Why It Matters
Obesity affects over 890 million people worldwide. Despite the success of GLP-1 agonists (Ozempic, Mounjaro), many patients don't respond or face side effects. Understanding the molecular switches that determine whether adipocytes store or burn energy could unlock entirely new therapeutic targets. This competition bridges computational prediction and wet-lab validation – the winning models will be tested experimentally at the Broad.
